Welcome to Wan Mei Travel Company

Your gateway to discovering the beauty and wonder of Kenya through exceptional travel experiences.

Founded with a passion for showcasing Kenya's incredible natural beauty and rich cultural heritage, Wan Mei Travel Company has been dedicated to creating unforgettable journeys for travelers from around the world. We believe that travel is more than just visiting places – it's about creating lasting memories, fostering cultural understanding, and experiencing the authentic spirit of Kenya.

We don't just arrange a trip, we are the guide to the wilderness story. From beast migrations in Tanzania to spectacular scenes from the Maasai Mara, with sustainable tourism as your starting point, take you to Africa's true heartbeat with an indigenous perspective. Every stop is a deep dialogue with nature; Every journey contains a reverence and love for life.

When the morning fog has not yet cleared, the 4x4 has driven into the heart of the grassland. See the zebras cross the Mala River like a black tide in a breathtaking "transit of the sky" and hear the lions growl in the golden sunset. We'll stay with you in the best seats, film the most spectacular survival epic on Earth, and finish with a champagne breakfast that lets the wilderness dance with the breeze and bubbles.

In Lake Nakuru and Lake Natron, millions of flamingos dye the lake's surface to a flowing pink, and the sound of their wings passing through the water is a natural melody. Turning to Amboseli, watch a family of elephants stroll through the grasslands against the cloud-shrouded tip of Kilimanjaro - a picture of the "five great Africans" in tandem with snow mountains that only a skilled guide knows how to capture precisely.

About Nairobi

Nairobi is situated in South-Central Kenya, 140 kilometers south of the Equator. The city is surrounded by 113 km² of plains, cliffs, and forest that make up Nairobi National Park. It's adjacent to the eastern edge of the Rift Valley, with the Ngong Hills to the west, Mount Kenya to the north, and Mount Kilimanjaro to the south-east.

As Kenya's capital and the most populous city in East Africa with approximately 3.5 million residents, Nairobi serves as the gateway for most visitors to Kenya. The city is well-connected by international airlines and regional road networks linking it to other major East African urban centers.
