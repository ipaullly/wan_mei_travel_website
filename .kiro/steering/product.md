# Product Overview

Wan Mei is a travel company website based in Nairobi, Kenya, offering tourism services including:

- Car hire and transfer services
- Safari tours
- Hotel bookings
- Travel packages

The website serves as a marketing platform showcasing the company's services with a focus on Kenyan tourism. It features location information about Nairobi, service offerings, and contact details for potential customers.

The site emphasizes visual appeal with parallax effects, image galleries, and a modern responsive design to attract tourists and travelers visiting Kenya.
